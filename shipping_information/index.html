Shipping Information

How long does it take for Supravi to ship? Does Supravi use UPS or FedEx?

Expedited delivery (3 business days) - Most orders are shipped UPS or USPS. Usually, UPS will ring your doorbell then leave the package in a sheltered area. USPS will deliver the package in your mailbox if it fits, or put it by your door.

Standard delivery (3-5 business days) - Excludes items shipped direct from the manufacturer. Estimated delivery times will be displayed in checkout. Orders are shipped UPS. UPS will ring your doorbell then leave the package in a sheltered area.

Expedited delivery (2 business days) - Excludes items shipped direct from the manufacturer. Estimated delivery times will be displayed in checkout. Orders are shipped UPS. UPS will ring your doorbell then leave the package in a sheltered area.

Expedited delivery (1 business day) - Excludes items shipped direct from the manufacturer. Estimated delivery times will be displayed in checkout. Orders are shipped UPS. UPS will ring your doorbell then leave the package in a sheltered area.

Store delivery - If you choose to have your item shipped to a Supravi store or Supravi express center, most orders are ready for pick-up in 4-7 business days. You will be contacted when your order arrives. Your order will be charged to your payment card or account at the time it is shipped. For your protection, please bring the payment card used for payment when picking up your order. Orders are held for 10 days only, so please call the store if you need extra time. After 10 days, merchandise is returned and your card or account credited for the purchase price of the items (shipping and handling charges are non-refundable). Find the JCP store nearest you with our store locator.

Does Supravi have free shipping?

Orders that qualify for free shipping are shipped our Standard Delivery 3-5 business day method. Eligible customers must select this option in checkout in order to receive free shipping. Free shipping excludes truck-delivered items and may also exclude select manufacturer delivered items based on participation.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST will be shipped the next business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST on Friday, Saturday, Sunday or holidays will be shipped the next business day.

What are estimated delivery dates?

The estimated delivery date is based on the selected shipping method. Shipping dates are based on business days, they do not include weekends.

When do we take payment?

We do not charge your credit card until your order has shipped. Additional shipping surcharges may apply to some large and heavy items. This amount is listed at the item level, on the individual item product, detail page, and in checkout. This item surcharge is in addition to standard shipping rates.

How do I check on an order from Supravi?

Sign in to your Supravi.com account to view your order history.
